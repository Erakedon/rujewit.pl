Your eight picks

The approved designs and the brand guide disagree in these places, or leave them open. Each option below is the real component. Reply with your picks by number, for example "2B, 6A, the rest recommended". The real pages already use the recommended options.

1 · Header at tablet widths (768–1023 px)

The approved header needs about 800 px, more than a tablet in portrait has.

1A Recommended Keep the full header, with less space at the sides and between the links (16 px each). It fits at 768 px in both languages and follows the brand rule "full logo from 768 px".

6 · QR code size

The approved slot is 148 px, and a bank QR code needs a white margin. These are sample codes, not payment codes.

6A Recommended The code itself is 148 px, and the white margin takes it to 180 px. It keeps the brand guide's "at least 148 px", and larger codes scan more easily.

7 · Spacing that's off the token scale

A few approved values aren't on the spacing scale: buttons 12/22 and 8/14, nav gap 28, card padding 22, header actions gap 18.

7A Recommended Move them to the nearest tokens: buttons 12/24 and 8/12, nav gap 24, card padding 24, actions gap 16. Small buttons use 12 instead of 16, because 16 doesn't fit a 320 px phone.

8 · Movement in the gród

The approved scene has no sky, smoke or birds, so the only parts that could move are the river's reflections.

8B A slow shimmer on the river. Any loop this long needs a pause button, and it's off for people who ask for reduced motion.
